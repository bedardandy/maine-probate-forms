## 18-A transition

MATERIAL DIFFERENCE: under former 18-A the elective share was a flat 1/3 of the augmented estate; under 18-C §2-202 it is 50% of the MARITAL-PROPERTY PORTION of the augmented estate, which scales with length of marriage (§2-203). Date of death selects which regime applies. Title 18-C applies to estates of decedents who die on or after 2019-09-01, and to any proceeding pending on or commenced after that date. For a decedent who died before 2019-09-01, former Title 18-A supplies the substantive succession rules even though the proceeding itself runs under 18-C. See _index/18a-key-diffs.json (governing rule: 18-C §8-301).

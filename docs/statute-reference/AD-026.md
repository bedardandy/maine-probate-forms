## Per-question considerations

Material questions on this form and the statutes to weigh when answering them.

### `consent_required` — Consent Required
- [18-C §9-302](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/18-C/title18-Csec9-302.html) (Consent for adoption): Adult adoption requires the adoptee's consent (and possibly a spouse's).

### `inheritance_acknowledgment` — Inheritance Acknowledgment
- [18-C §9-105](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/18-C/title18-Csec9-105.html) (Rights of adopted persons): Adoption changes inheritance rights.
- [18-C §2-117](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/18-C/title18-Csec2-117.html) (Effect of an order granting adoption on adoptee and adoptee's former parents): Effect of an adoption order on inheritance from/through former and adoptive parents.

### `birth_parents_inheritance_request` — Birth Parents Inheritance Request
- [18-C §2-117](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/18-C/title18-Csec2-117.html) (Effect of an order granting adoption on adoptee and adoptee's former parents): Whether birth-parent inheritance is preserved is governed by §2-117.

## 18-A transition

18-C Article 9 governs adoption proceedings under the current code; §9-108 addresses application of prior laws to pending matters.

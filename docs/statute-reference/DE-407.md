*Renunciation of priority and nomination of personal representative.*

**Applies:** Title 18-C (Maine Uniform Probate Code), effective 2019-09-01 — see transition_18a.

## Governing statutes

- **[18-C §3-203](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/18-C/title18-Csec3-203.html)** — Priority among persons seeking appointment as personal representative  
  A person with priority may renounce and nominate another to serve as PR.

## 18-A transition

Title 18-C applies to estates of decedents who die on or after 2019-09-01, and to any proceeding pending on or commenced after that date. For a decedent who died before 2019-09-01, former Title 18-A supplies the substantive succession rules even though the proceeding itself runs under 18-C.

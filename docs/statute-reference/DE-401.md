## 18-A transition

Estate-tax exposure is governed by Title 36, Chapter 577 (deaths after 2012); the legacy 18-A-era cite to 36 M.R.S. §4063 is superseded. Title 18-C applies to estates of decedents who die on or after 2019-09-01, and to any proceeding pending on or commenced after that date. For a decedent who died before 2019-09-01, former Title 18-A supplies the substantive succession rules even though the proceeding itself runs under 18-C.

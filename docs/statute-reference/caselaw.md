# Maine Law Court — Estate & Probate Cases to Consider

> **Statutes for consideration — not legal advice.** This page lists statutes an LLM or person filling the form may want to *consider*; it does not tell you what to do or conclude. It is not a substitute for a licensed Maine attorney. Which code applies can turn on the date of death — see the transition note. Verify everything against the current statute.

Curated, verified Maine Law Court decisions relevant to the probate forms. Cases are tied to forms by the statute(s) they bear on; 18-A-era decisions are mapped forward to the current 18-C section that carries the same rule (noted per case). 'holding_source': 'primary' = holding read from the opinion text; 'secondary' = case existence and citation confirmed from an authoritative reporter/aggregator (Justia/courts.maine.gov) and the holding stated at the level of well-established, corroborated Maine doctrine. Verify against the opinion before relying on it.

Each case is tied to forms through the statute(s) it bears on (see the per-form pages). 18-A-era decisions are mapped forward to the current 18-C section carrying the same rule.

## [Adult Guardianship and Conservatorship of T., 2022 ME 51](https://www.courts.maine.gov/courts/sjc/lawcourt/2022/22me051.pdf) (2022)

- **Topic:** Adult guardianship/conservatorship — unified proceeding
- **Decided under:** 18-C
- **Bears on:** 18-C §5-301, 18-C §5-401, 18-C §5-312, 18-C §5-413
- **Tied to forms:** [PP-107](PP-107.md), [PP-201](PP-201.md), [PP-205](PP-205.md), [PP-401](PP-401.md), [PP-410](PP-410.md), [PP-507](PP-507.md)
- **Holding (read from the opinion):** Appointing DHHS as an adult's full guardian and conservator under 18-C M.R.S. §§ 5-301 and 5-401(2): the guardianship statutes create a single 'unified proceeding,' so a court may consider evidence admitted at an earlier emergency hearing when it rules after the final hearing.

## [Adult Guardianship and Conservatorship of R., 2026 ME 43](https://law.justia.com/cases/maine/supreme-court/2026/2026-me-43.html) (2026)

- **Topic:** Guardianship/conservatorship — right to effective assistance of counsel; termination
- **Decided under:** 18-C
- **Bears on:** 18-C §5-305, 18-C §5-319, 18-C §5-401, 18-C §5-431
- **Tied to forms:** [PP-107](PP-107.md), [PP-201](PP-201.md), [PP-205](PP-205.md), [PP-209](PP-209.md), [PP-401](PP-401.md), [PP-413](PP-413.md), [PP-502](PP-502.md), [PP-503](PP-503.md), [PP-506](PP-506.md)
- **Holding (read from the opinion):** Affirming the denial of a protected person's petition to terminate his guardianship and the appointment of DHHS as conservator; the Court recognized for the first time that a person subject to guardianship or conservatorship proceedings has a right to the effective assistance of counsel, and articulated a procedure for challenging an order on that ground.

## [In re Estate of Hiller, 2014 ME 2](https://law.justia.com/cases/maine/supreme-court/2014/2014-me-2.html) (2014)

- **Topic:** Personal representative — breach of fiduciary duty; removal/surcharge
- **Decided under:** 18-A
- **Bears on:** 18-C §3-611, 18-C §3-712, 18-C §3-703
- **Tied to forms:** [DE-509](DE-509.md)
- **Holding (from published summaries — verify against the opinion):** A personal representative who wrongfully depleted estate assets breached the fiduciary duty owed to the estate; such conduct supports removal and a surcharge. (Decided under former 18-A; the fiduciary-duty and removal rules continue in 18-C §§3-703, 3-711/3-712 and 3-611.)

## [Estate of Bonin, 457 A.2d 1123](https://law.justia.com/cases/maine/supreme-court/1983/457-a-2d-1123-0.html) (1983)

- **Topic:** Personal representative — removal and surcharge for failure of duty
- **Decided under:** 18-A
- **Bears on:** 18-C §3-611, 18-C §3-712
- **Tied to forms:** [DE-509](DE-509.md)
- **Holding (from published summaries — verify against the opinion):** A personal representative was removed and surcharged for failing fiduciary duties (including lost interest from not depositing proceeds in interest-bearing accounts and refund of excessive fees). (Former 18-A; principle continues in 18-C §§3-712, 3-611.)

## [In re Estate of Kruzynski, 2000 ME 17](https://law.justia.com/cases/maine/supreme-court/2000/2000-me-17-0.html) (2000)

- **Topic:** Three-year ultimate time limit to commence probate/appointment
- **Decided under:** 18-A
- **Bears on:** 18-C §3-108
- **Tied to forms:** [DE-101](DE-101.md), [DE-201](DE-201.md), [DE-507](DE-507.md)
- **Holding (from published summaries — verify against the opinion):** No informal or formal probate or appointment proceeding may be commenced more than three years after the decedent's death (the ultimate time limit). (Former 18-A §3-108; carried forward verbatim in substance to 18-C §3-108.)

## [Estate of Staples, 672 A.2d 99](https://law.justia.com/cases/maine/supreme-court/1996/672-a-2d-99-0.html) (1996)

- **Topic:** Creditors' claims — nonclaim bar
- **Decided under:** 18-A
- **Bears on:** 18-C §3-803
- **Tied to forms:** [DE-101](DE-101.md), [DE-201](DE-201.md), [DE-503](DE-503.md)
- **Holding (from published summaries — verify against the opinion):** The nonclaim statute bars payment of a creditor claim that was not presented and pursued within the statutory period; a court cannot authorize payment of a claim already barred. (Former 18-A; the nonclaim limitations continue in 18-C §3-803.)

## [In re Estate of Lewis, 2001 ME 74](https://law.justia.com/cases/maine/supreme-court/2001/2001-me-74-0.html) (2001)

- **Topic:** Will contest — undue influence; clear and convincing evidence
- **Decided under:** 18-A
- **Bears on:** 18-C §2-501, 18-C §3-407
- **Tied to forms:** [DE-201](DE-201.md)
- **Holding (from published summaries — verify against the opinion):** Undue influence sufficient to invalidate a will must be established by clear and convincing evidence; a confidential relationship between the testator and a beneficiary can affect the analysis. (Will-validity and contested-testacy principles; in 18-C, contested testacy and burdens are addressed in §§3-401 to 3-407.)

## [Estate of Record, 534 A.2d 1319](https://law.justia.com/cases/maine/supreme-court/1987/534-a-2d-1319-0.html) (1987)

- **Topic:** Will contest — burden of proof on capacity and undue influence
- **Decided under:** 18-A
- **Bears on:** 18-C §2-501, 18-C §3-407
- **Tied to forms:** [DE-201](DE-201.md)
- **Holding (from published summaries — verify against the opinion):** The party contesting a will offered for probate bears the burden of proving either the decedent's lack of testamentary capacity or the exertion of undue influence. (Burden allocation reflected in 18-C §3-407.)

## [Estate of Dodge, 576 A.2d 755](https://law.justia.com/cases/maine/supreme-court/1990/576-a-2d-755-0.html) (1990)

- **Topic:** Will contest — undue influence; clear and convincing evidence
- **Decided under:** 18-A
- **Bears on:** 18-C §2-501, 18-C §3-407
- **Tied to forms:** [DE-201](DE-201.md)
- **Holding (from published summaries — verify against the opinion):** Undue influence must be proven by clear and convincing evidence. (Will-validity principle; contested-testacy procedure now in 18-C §§3-401 to 3-407.)

## [Estate of Fisher, 545 A.2d 1266](https://law.justia.com/cases/maine/supreme-court/1988/545-a-2d-1266-0.html) (1988)

- **Topic:** Elective share — sources and valuation
- **Decided under:** 18-A
- **Bears on:** 18-C §2-209
- **Tied to forms:** [DE-506](DE-506.md)
- **Holding (from published summaries — verify against the opinion):** In satisfying the surviving spouse's elective share, the court must determine the sources from which the share is payable and value property passing to the spouse that is charged against the share. NOTE: decided under the former 18-A elective-share regime (flat 1/3 of the augmented estate); 18-C replaced that with 50% of the marital-property portion (§2-202/§2-203), so the computation differs for deaths on/after 2019-09-01.

## [Estate of Galluzzo, 615 A.2d 236](https://law.justia.com/cases/maine/supreme-court/1992/615-a-2d-236-0.html) (1992)

- **Topic:** Elective share — augmented estate; timing of petition
- **Decided under:** 18-A
- **Bears on:** 18-C §2-202, 18-C §2-211
- **Tied to forms:** [DE-506](DE-506.md)
- **Holding (from published summaries — verify against the opinion):** Addressed what property is included in the augmented estate and the elective-share petition in an estate. NOTE: decided under the former 18-A flat-1/3 elective-share regime; the 18-C marital-property-portion approach (§2-202/§2-203) governs deaths on/after 2019-09-01, so inclusion/valuation analysis differs.

# N-118 — Notice of Guardianship Conservatorship Proceeding

*Notice of guardianship/conservatorship proceeding and post-appointment events.*

> **Statutes for consideration — not legal advice.** This page lists statutes an LLM or person filling the form may want to *consider*; it does not tell you what to do or conclude. It is not a substitute for a licensed Maine attorney. Which code applies can turn on the date of death — see the transition note. Verify everything against the current statute.

**Applies:** Title 18-C (Maine Uniform Probate Code), effective 2019-09-01 — see transition_18a.

## Per-question considerations

Material questions on this form and the statutes to weigh when answering them.

### `change_in_permanent_dwelling` — Change In Permanent Dwelling
- [18-C §5-315](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/18-C/title18-Csec5-315.html) (Special limitations on guardian's power): Moving the protected person's dwelling triggers notice/limits on guardian power.

### `restrictions_on_contact` — Restrictions On Contact
- [18-C §5-315](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/18-C/title18-Csec5-315.html) (Special limitations on guardian's power): Restricting contact with others is a specially limited guardian power requiring notice.

### `revised_guardianship_plan_filed` — Revised Guardianship Plan Filed
- [18-C §5-316](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/18-C/title18-Csec5-316.html) (Guardian's plan): A revised guardian's plan must be filed/approved.

### `conservators_report_and_accounting` — Conservators Report And Accounting
- [18-C §5-423](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/18-C/title18-Csec5-423.html) (Conservator's report and accounting; monitoring): Conservator's report and accounting is filed and noticed.

## 18-A transition

18-C Article 5 (Maine Uniform Guardianship, Conservatorship and Protective Proceedings Act) governs proceedings pending on or commenced after 2019-09-01. Orders entered under former Title 18-A may rest on different standards; the modern least-restrictive / supported-decision-making framework applies going forward. See _index/18a-key-diffs.json.

## 18-A transition

18-C Article 5 (Maine Uniform Guardianship, Conservatorship and Protective Proceedings Act) governs proceedings pending on or commenced after 2019-09-01. Orders entered under former Title 18-A may rest on different standards; the modern least-restrictive / supported-decision-making framework applies going forward. See _index/18a-key-diffs.json.

  Affirming the denial of a protected person's petition to terminate his guardianship and the appointment of DHHS as conservator; the Court recognized for the first time that a person subject to guardianship or conservatorship proceedings has a right to the effective assistance of counsel, and articulated a procedure for challenging an order on that ground.
